# [Why I built this]
While practicing Japanese typing for my future life and professional career in Tokyo, I felt the need for a more personalized training method. I wanted to practice with texts that truly resonate with me—specifically, the lyrics of my favorite Showa-era songs.
By creating this tool, I am able to customize my own training data, allowing me to master Japanese typing while immersing myself in the cultural atmosphere I love. This project is my way of combining my passion with the essential skills I need to succeed in a professional Japanese environment.

# [Goals]
Beyond practicing lyrics, I plan to expand this tool to include various business documents, ensuring I am fully prepared for the professional demands of a workplace in Tokyo.
